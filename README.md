## 📖 About The Game

VICE HEARTS is my first Python game inspired by GTA Vice City. You start with $5000 and $100 Health. You can choose your own path - become a honest worker or become a criminal. Every choice changes your money and health.

Your progress is automatically saved, so you can close the game and continue later from the same money.

---

## ✨ Features

- 💰 **Money System** - Earn and spend money in real time
- ❤️ **Health System** - Police attacks decrease your health
- ⭐ **Wanted System** - Police chase you with star levels
- 🏦 **Bank Robbery Mission** - Earn $6000 in one mission
- 🚕 **Job Mission** - Earn $1500 by honest taxi job
- 💸 **Rob People Mission** - Earn $2000 by robbing people on street
- 🏥 **Hospital System** - Restore full health for $500
- 💾 **Auto Save System** - Your money is saved in `Save.txt`
